About MIRÁ

We are the ones we have been waiting for.

A Hopi prophecy. We open our presentation with it because it sums up what we believe: change starts with us.

Who we are we, the people

MIRÁ comes from Tupi-Guarani and means “we, the people”. We believe that human beings are the catalyst of change, and that the best way to change the world is to strengthen our human systems: our organizations.

We support the organizational development of businesses, institutions and initiatives that pursue prosperity without giving up on generating a positive impact on the human, environmental and cultural ecosystem. Organizations that want to balance results with care for relationships, and find happiness at work.

Our purpose is to co-create the planet we dream of.

The essence of MIRÁ

Human

We create light, warm and welcoming environments, the opposite of the ceremonious facilitation that keeps people at a distance. Lightness shows up in the materials, in the way we speak and listen, and in the activities. Light environments do not replace rigor: they are what opens space for people to give themselves to the process.

Deep

We combine lightness and depth. We design processes so people truly talk to each other, in small groups. We dive in together, rather than directing from the outside. We focus the workshop on what needs the whole group and save the details for refinement sessions. Every activity exists to serve a real goal.

Diverse

Diversity is the foundation of quality participatory processes. We hold a permanent commitment to the LGBTQIA+ community and, as women, to confronting sexism in dialogue. We adapt language, tools and methodology to include those usually left at the margins: women, young people, Indigenous peoples, traditional communities, quilombolas and people who cannot read or write. And we map the group's profile so the report shows the diversity present, or its absence.

Rooted

We have facilitated many processes in villages, conservation units and extractive reserves, with our feet on the ground. Every meeting is anchored in what really matters to those people and that context. We move between the tables and build together: MIRÁ means “we, the people”.

Artisanal

We build every workshop by hand, together with the client, without tying ourselves to a single methodology. We prepare high-quality visual materials, from the agenda to the event's digital guide. We use technology, including artificial intelligence, to speed things up, never to replace the human eye.

Systemic

We see organizations holistically, through the 7 dimensions of Organization Design®. Development is a continuous spiral, so we don't work with isolated solutions. We combine our methodological lens with the knowledge of those who live each context, including its political and social complexities. Discover Organization Design®

Regenerative

We work alongside socio-environmental organizations, Indigenous peoples, traditional communities, public agencies and conservation initiatives. We favor digital solutions, cut down on printing and disposables, and value local suppliers. We invite short pauses of silence and reconnection with oneself, with others and with nature.

Consulting

Consulting, to transform

For when you want to transform your organization from within and have support along the way. We accompany projects, companies, NGOs, collectives, schools and communities, from strategic planning to executive secretariat.

Strategic Planning

We help your organization define clearly where it wants to go, and stay on course. We design a tailor-made methodology, with approaches such as Theory of Change, Transformative Scenarios, PESTEL, Appreciative Inquiry and the Balanced Scorecard.

The bigger challenge is sustaining the strategy: we deliver monitoring tools and facilitate periodic reviews, so the plan doesn't end up in a drawer.

Organizational Development

While planning defines the “where to”, organizational development takes care of “how” the organization works on the inside: decisions, agreements, meetings, team management and communication.

We don't implement ready-made models. It's long-haul work that brings fluidity, clarity, better results and more happiness in everyday life.

FIT Consulting

Integral Happiness at Work (FIT), for small organizations that don't usually have access to consulting. Inspired by Gross National Happiness, it puts happiness at the center of strategy, built on three elements: working with lightness, having more time, and financial sustainability.

It includes awareness-raising, a diagnosis of the 7 dimensions, access to the FIT Journey, implementation workshops and mentoring.

Visual recording

We draw in real time what is being said and built, on paper or digital panels. The record makes participants feel heard, supports shared understanding, makes dialogue visible and creates an engaging synthesis.

We prepare the graphic design before the event, in the organization's visual identity.

Reporting
